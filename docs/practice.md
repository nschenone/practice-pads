# Practicing with Practice Pads

In Guided practice, select a parent tonic and family, then choose one of its seven modes. The selected mode has its own tonic and literal seventh chord, but its notes come from the parent. Switching modes retains the selected physical position. Choose Play to start the pad; Stop releases it. Adjust the volume before listening, and try the five synthesized sounds at a low level.

For D jazz melodic minor, select degree III to hear Fmaj7#5 and see F Lydian augmented: F G A B C# D E. B is the #4 and C# is the #5 relative to F. C# also belongs to the chord F A C# E. The parent remains D jazz melodic minor, not F major.

In Self-guided practice, choose a root and chord quality. The list contains modes whose pitch classes include the chord tones. It is a starting point for listening, not a guarantee that a mode fits a particular progression.

## Guitar positions

The major diagram uses fretboard.js's five major-derived templates. They have not been teacher-reviewed. A position describes physical locations; fingering specifies fingers, an ordered exercise specifies a sequence, and phrasing concerns how notes are played in time. One does not automatically supply the others.

Natural minor is degree VI of its relative major. Its major-derived positions show the same pitch collection, not necessarily five named minor chord forms. Jazz melodic minor and harmonic minor have different notes and need their own treatment.

The next implementation intends direct C, A, G, E and D position buttons, a compact practice area and warm-paper notation surfaces. For minor families, the intended fallback is a library scale note map, explicitly not CAGED. A small local editor is planned per parent, family and position, with unreviewed defaults, Tonal validation, browser local storage, import/export and reset. These changes are pending integration and final verification; do not assume they are available or tested in this version. Local storage is browser-specific, not cloud sync or a backup.

Musician review of positions, notation and exercises remains necessary. See the [visualization reference](visualization.md) and [CAGED research](caged-research.md).
